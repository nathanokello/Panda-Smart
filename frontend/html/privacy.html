How Panda Smart uses your information

This notice is written in plain language. Items marked [to confirm] must be settled by the team before a pilot with real farmers.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and ward.
  • The approximate size of your plots (not their exact location).
  • Your planting plans, and grain lot records: weight, moisture, check-ups, losses and corrections.
  • Whether you confirmed or disputed a lot record, and your reason.
  • The date you gave consent.

What we do not collect

  • Voice recordings or voice prints. Voice is never used to identify you.
  • Exact farm GPS location.
  • Payment or bank details. Panda Smart does not hold or move money.

Why

To keep a dated record of your stored grain that you can show at sale, and to help your ward plan planting.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own farmer record, plots and planting plans.
  • Storage agents and cooperative admins see records of farmers in their own cooperative only.
  • Ward dashboards show totals only, and hide any crop with fewer than 5 farmers in a ward.
  • The link sent to you to confirm a lot shows that one lot only. It expires after 14 days.
